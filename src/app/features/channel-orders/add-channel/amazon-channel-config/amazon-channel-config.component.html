How to Connect Amazon?

  • Step 1: Follow below steps to generate Amazon Merchant Token.
  • Step 2: Login to your Amazon seller account using registered email with Amazon and corresponding password.
  • Step 3: Settings > Account Info > Business Information, and then click on Merchant Token.
  • Step 4: Click on Connect to establish connection of Parcelx with your Amazon account.
  • Step 5: Once connection is successful click on Save and next to update necessary fulfillment details.
